V7 municipal and downtown tax-treatment maps

The maps share three colors: blue-gray for Town property tax billed (including partial relief), dark teal for verified full exemption, and gold for unresolved, mixed or other footprints. Gray outside the municipal line is outside Town, not a Town exemption. On the downtown map, the lighter background outside the study is unclassified context. The regional overview retains its original jurisdiction colors and is clearly labeled as geographic rather than exemption mapping.

The frozen August 4 records reconcile to 4,472 complete assessment IDs: 4,124 with a positive PAID/UNPAID Town levy; 183 verified fully exempt; and 165 other (160 released-only and five centrally assessed utilities). The earlier 188 no-positive-levy group includes five utilities and must not be described as 188 verified full exemptions. Full exemption requires an explicit assessment exemption covering the positive gross assessment, no positive active Town levy, and no conflicting taxable active bill. Released bills do not establish exemption. Billed does not mean collected.

All full assessment IDs, including condo and lease suffixes, remain intact. Billing rows are grouped by complete ID; any positive active Town bill places that record in the billed category. Records are counted once, never once per building row or polygon overlap. Exact GIS matches cover 4,441 IDs: 4,109 billed, 182 exempt and 150 other. The 31 unmatched IDs comprise 15 billed, one exempt and 15 other. A prior countywide exact-PIN query found no matches; no successor footprints are invented.

Eight existing base identifiers associated with missing suffix accounts are conservatively shown as other, without assigning those absent accounts a geometry. GIS footprints with different category assignments overlap in 77 pairs. Their intersecting land (union, not summed pairs) is shown gold; this includes parent/condo and other coincident geometry conflicts. Same-category overlaps retain that category. These are mapping ambiguities, not new legal tax classifications or an allocation of condo ownership shares. Features with no exact Town assessment record are also other inside the boundary. Differences between historical assessment IDs and current GIS features must not be interpreted as new parcel totals.

The complete October 19 municipal geometry is unchanged from v6: 19 features, 18 nonempty, 21 union parts including small slivers. The full municipal extent plus 600 feet contains 7,913 distinct GIS PINs and 1,437 road features. All query batches were checked for completeness. 4,485 GIS features intersect Town by more than one square foot. Before the mixed-area overlay, these are 4,101 billed, 182 exempt and 202 other. These feature counts differ from assessment-record counts and do not measure unique land area. Outside portions of crossing polygons remain gray. Very small crossings may reflect GIS alignment.

Saved-source checks confirm USPS (9874161145), County courthouse (9874058688), old courthouse (9874066106), Town cemetery (9874190573), Town campus (9874088727) and UNC hospital (9873320287) are verified exempt and have no mixed overlay. The planned train-station site and planned fire headquarters remain described as planned; completion is not inferred. South Churton and numbered landmark anchors are retained. The approved downtown study, Town Center future-land-use outline, municipal boundary and official historic district are distinct geographies; the downtown study is not the official historic district.

Main-page wording

“No Town tax shown” describes the saved local records, not a finding that every property is legally fully exempt or owes no tax of any kind. Partial reductions may apply to records that still have Town tax billed. “Property tax records” preserves complete assessment IDs, including suffixes; it does not imply a one-to-one count of distinct parcel footprints. The approved downtown study boundary is different from both the municipal boundary and the official Historic District. Completion of the planned fire headquarters has not been verified.

Town billing and exemptions

4,124 positive-levy records + 93 zero-levy active bills + 95 explicit fully exempt assessment-only records + 160 released-only records = 4,472. The 188 records without a positive local levy carry $460.8M appraisal, or 17.4% of Town-cohort appraisal. Five are utility records with unresolved central taxation; do not describe the whole group as legally fully exempt. The older overview used the broader fully exempt label; this preview makes that limitation explicit. Active Town levy is $11,062,030; released-only nominal appraisal totals $559. No current payment or delinquency conclusion is drawn. Personal/business property, vehicles, central utilities and split jurisdictions require tax-abstract reconciliation.

Historic-property check

No confirmed HIS-coded recipient or recorded deferral appears within the approved 805-PIN list. This does not prove that every form of historic-property relief is absent: the 27 partial exclusions have blank codes. Nine exact 50% reductions cannot establish a historic program by themselves. Three known HIS records—Eno Cotton Mill units 1 and 10, and Bellevue Mill—are outside this study. Historic Hillsborough Commission at 319 N. Churton is fully exempt, not a demonstrated 50% historic deferral.

Town Ledger’s expanded downtown study area is the approved 805-record boundary. Town-designated Town Center is the official future-land-use area; neither label means the municipal boundary or Historic District.
