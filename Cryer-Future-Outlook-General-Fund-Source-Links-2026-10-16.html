What drives the gap?

Personnel & operations

Personnel and operations account for about 93% of the increase in net spending from FY2029 to FY2039 in this scenario.

Projects & debt

Annual spending includes the Town’s cash contributions and loan repayments. Full project budgets are shown on the linked detail page.

Revenue growth

Revenue grows much more slowly than spending in this scenario.

Sources & how to read this

Years shown. FY2025 actuals as presented in the FY2027 recommended budget; FY2026 estimate; FY2027 recommended budget; FY2028–2029 Town projections; FY2030–2039 Town Ledger scenario.

Sources and method. The Town’s FY2027 Recommended Budget, General Fund financial summary (p. 20), and Town-supplied Debt Service Projections.xlsx. The Cryer v52 model extends these figures using the assumptions shown above. Utility reimbursements are deducted from both funding and spending. Debt payments are included in spending.

Reserve target. The Town’s policy sets a reserve target range of 33%–100% of annual General Fund operating expenses. The 33% figure is the lower end of that range. This preview estimates it at $7.2M by taking 33% of the $21.9M expense total used in the Town’s FY2029 financial summary. That total includes operating costs, debt payments, capital purchases and transfers to other funds, less utility reimbursements. Source: FY2027 Recommended Budget, pp. 18 and 20.

Potential cushion. The Town projects a General Fund balance of $11.8M at the end of FY2029. We still need to confirm how much of that balance is uncommitted and available under the reserve policy. The $4.6M above the estimated reserve target is therefore a potential cushion; it is not a confirmed amount available to spend.

Loans and timing. The Town’s debt schedule separates loans already taken out (“Existing loans”) from planned borrowing (“Future loans”). A–E identify the five future loans and mark when payments begin. These are payment dates, not construction start dates. FY2025 and FY2026 show total debt payments; loan-by-loan details begin in FY2027. The chart includes only the loans in that schedule.

Cumulative figures. This row adds each year’s surplus or shortfall, starting in FY2025. Savings accumulated before FY2025 are excluded. The separate savings section shows the Town’s projected FY2029 fund balance. Totals are calculated before rounding; chart amounts are shown to one decimal place in millions. Dollar amounts are not adjusted for inflation.
