Sources & how to read this

Project funding. Project costs and cash contributions come from the Town’s FY2027–FY2033 Capital Improvements Plan preserved in Cryer v52. Project estimates combine the current project budget and planned project outlays through FY2033, excluding identified running costs. They are not complete lifetime costs. Current project budgets are budget amounts, not proof of money already spent. Borrowing and repayments follow the debt schedule except where a card identifies a capital-plan figure. Project running costs are separate and are not fully specified for every project.

Payment schedule. Payments come from the Town-supplied Debt Service Projections.xlsx, preserved in the Cryer v52 model. The Debt sheet lists annual payments for these 17 Water & Sewer loans. Their payments add up to the Town’s totals for every year shown.

Years covered. The utility overview shows total funding and spending through FY2029, and debt payments through FY2039. This loan schedule covers FY2027–FY2048. From FY2030 onward, the figures here show debt payments only. They do not show the utility’s total spending or any gap between funding and spending.

Existing and future loans. The Town’s schedule groups payments into existing loans and planned borrowing. This page follows those two groups. It includes only the loans in that schedule. A zero means no payment is shown for that loan in the selected year. “Last scheduled payment” means the last year with a payment in the supplied schedule.

Loan assumptions. The Projected Assumptions sheet gives planned borrowing amounts, interest rates and terms for nine of the ten future loans. Dam Repairs has a payment schedule but no matching loan assumptions. The amounts here follow the annual payment schedule; they are not recalculated from the assumptions.

Project names and timing. Full Town project names and source cells appear on the cards. Existing loans began before this schedule starts. Their year counts show the remaining payment schedule from FY2027, not their original loan terms. The first payment year for a future loan is not its construction start year. The schedule does not fully describe what each existing loan paid for.

Amounts. Payments include principal and interest together; the source does not separate them by year. Dollar amounts are not adjusted for inflation. For FY2027–FY2029, these payments are included in the Water & Sewer spending totals in the overview.
